# Encore concept films: working rules

This repo holds Encore's unofficial game-concept films for web3 projects, one folder per project under `videos/<handle>/`.

## Every delivery includes an outreach brief

Every new or updated film gets a `videos/<handle>/OUTREACH.md` that **grokbot** uses to write the outreach DM or post. Also paste the brief's two-line description and a short version of the brief into the final chat message, so the user can hand it over without opening the repo. `videos/tosheeznft/OUTREACH.md` is the reference example.

Use these sections, in this order:

1. **Target:** project name, X handle, website, what it is in their own words, 3–6 quotable lore lines, related accounts (with roles only if a source states them), and public status (mint/WL facts exactly as they posted them).
2. **What we made:** game name, the one-line pitch, how it plays, why it's theirs (lore → mechanic), how the art was made, what the film shows, and the video link (branch link now, `main` link after merge).
3. **Two-line description:**
   - Line 1: "[GAME NAME] - you [verb] [goal] in [their world], built from your own art."
   - Line 2: "Holders climb a WL leaderboard and share their result, bots can like a post but they can't [skill]."
4. **Angles grokbot can use:** 3–4 hooks tied to their lore or launch moment.
5. **About Encore:** what Encore is, the slogan "Every launch deserves an encore.", the credentials from Encore's own brief, encorestudio.xyz · @EncoreStudiohq, and the one ask: "Book a call".
6. **Voice rules:** from the Encore Brand Kit. Short, show first, real numbers never promises, at most one stage word.
7. **Never:** no "client" wording (it's an "unofficial concept"), no implied endorsement, no hype words, no prices or fake urgency, no mint/WL/price promises, sample leaderboards and post mock-ups are not real, and don't claim the game is already built.

Only use facts from opened sources (their posts, site, the brand kit). If something can't be found, leave it out rather than guess.

## Branding

The Encore end card follows the Encore Brand Kit (https://claude.ai/artifact/4FTN1wXXQ3AKYR4oUt5vVp):

- Black stage, the chrome lockup, Geist and Geist Mono type.
- "Every launch deserves an encore." with "an encore." in red (#EE3B43).
- One red thing per frame.
- CTA "Book a call", labelled "Unofficial concept".
- The old six-pill mark, "One crew for your whole launch." and "free 30-min call" are retired.
