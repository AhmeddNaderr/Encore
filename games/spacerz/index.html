    How to fly

    Fly
    W A S D or arrow keys. On touch, drag on the left side of the screen.
    Shoot
    Your blaster fires on its own. Aim with the mouse. On touch it aims at the closest enemy.
    Dash
    Space or Shift. Nothing can hit you mid-dash, and you ram whatever you fly through.
    Nova Well
    E or right-click when the bar is full. A black hole eats bullets and pulls enemies in, then blows.
    Mods
    Pick up cyan shards to level up, then choose one of three mods. They stack.
    Tickets
    Gold tickets are worth big points. Chain kills for a combo. Taking a hit resets it.
