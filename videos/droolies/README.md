# Droolies · VERY SMART

An unofficial Encore Studio game concept built entirely from Droolies' own art (@droolies_rh: "cross-eyed. drooling. very smart.").

**How it plays:**
- One eye aims; the other eye wanders "wherever it wants".
- Tap where the two sight-lines cross to pop only the *wrong* maths (2+3=6).
- Very-smart pops slurp your drool back up. Popping a correct sum makes you TOO NORMAL.
- If your drool hits the desk, it's SPLAT. Six in a row and your EYES POP OUT.
