# Spacerz: Forgotten Worlds

A 48-second unofficial gameplay trailer by Encore Studio for the playable browser game in [`games/spacerz`](../../games/spacerz).

- [Download the 1080p video](https://github.com/AhmeddNaderr/Encore/raw/refs/heads/main/videos/spacerz/Spacerz-Forgotten-Worlds-trailer.mp4)
- [Download the smaller 720p video](https://github.com/AhmeddNaderr/Encore/raw/refs/heads/main/videos/spacerz/Spacerz-Forgotten-Worlds-trailer-720p.mp4)

Both files contain H.264 video at 60fps and stereo AAC audio, with faststart metadata.

Every gameplay shot is real footage from the game, not a mock-up. The game ran on a controlled clock and was captured frame by frame, with an autopilot flying the Spacer. The music and sound effects are the game's own Web Audio synthesis, rendered offline and synced to the events in the footage.

Shots: title screen, a lineup of 30 of the 150 Spacerz, combat in Ember Mesa, a mod pick, the Nova Well, The Warden boss, a warp to Slime Hollow, the Comet Serpent boss, and an end card.

Art direction follows the Spacerz art on [@SpacerzNFT](https://x.com/SpacerzNFT): the cat-eared helmet silhouette, skull-faced survivors, and the dusky plum, coral and cream palette. The Spacer sprites are Encore Studio's own pixel versions in that style, not the original NFT images.

Unofficial fan concept by Encore Studio. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Spacerz. Spacerz and its original artwork belong to their respective owners.
