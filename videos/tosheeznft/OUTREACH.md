## What we made

- **Game:** *Tosheez: Nobody Knows*, an unofficial concept by Encore.
- **One line:** You hide Satoshi in plain sight among 7,021 Tosheez who all share his face.
- **How it plays:** Trends sweep the crowd ("Hard hats are in."). The Satoshi Hunter's magnifying glass chases whoever is off-trend. You swap Satoshi's hat, eyes and outfit to blend in before it reaches him. Then come double trends, fake-outs ("JK. Leather is in."), a second hunter, and a secret ₿ trait that triggers SPLIT: Satoshi copies his look onto other Tosheez, and the hunter can't tell which one is him.
- **Why it's theirs:** the game *is* their lore. Every Tosheez has Satoshi's face, so the collection itself is his disguise. SPLIT is their "split his footprint into thousands of styles" line made playable.
- **The art:** built only from their own hand-drawn traits. We separated their real trait art into 34 layers and recombined them; there is no AI art and no redraw.
- **The film:** 44 seconds, 1080p, 60 fps.
  - Opens mid-game.
  - "Last seen in 2011." → "Satoshi returns."
  - Pulls back into a crowd of hundreds of Tosheez.
  - Gameplay, then a "FOUND." fail.
  - A WL leaderboard with sample names, a shareable result card, then the Encore end card.
  - Original music.
