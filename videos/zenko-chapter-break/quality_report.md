# ZENKO: CHAPTER BREAK — quality report

These scores are self-review judgements tied to frames I opened. They are not founder approval. I could not watch the video at full speed; motion was judged from per-frame stills, contact sheets of the decoded MP4 and automated checks.

| Requirement | Score | Evidence / limits |
|---|---:|---|
| Art fidelity | 9 | Every character pixel is Zenko's own art: the banner Shonen, Shojo, Nero, the katana and scythe portraits, the official manga page, the card frame and the card backs. Corrupted panels are filters of the same art and restore to the original. Faces are not redrawn. |
| Originality | 9 | Scroll-through-the-manga combat with four gesture verbs, tag swaps, a card hand, a holder-poll plot fork and a splash-page finisher. It is built from Zenko's own roadmap (vertical-scroll manga, TCG, plot forks), not a generic reskin. |
| Interactivity | 9 | Swipe, tap, timed parry, hold-and-drag, tag swap, card drag, route choice and pinch all appear on screen with a visible finger, prompts and a controls legend that lights up the current gesture. |
| Hook | 8 | Opens mid-boss: Nero's piercing strike, "PINCH FOR SPLASH PAGE!", then the full-screen splash page. No logo first. Not tested on a cold audience. |
| Clarity (no sound) | 8 | Captions name each verb ("SWIPE TO SLASH", "TAP TO PARRY", "DRAG NERO THROUGH WALLS", "PICK THE PLOT FORK", "DRAG A CARD TO CAST"), and the minimap shows progress down the chapter. The main cut is dense; the 15 s cut is fast. |
| Motion | 8 (provisional) | 60 fps deterministic animation with camera scroll, knockback, hit flashes, shake and impact frames. Squash, stretch and acting are limited because the official art is used as cut-outs. |
| Polish | 8 | Katakana SFX, combo pops, damage numbers, the card frame in the HUD, the boss HP bar, "RESTORED" stamps, the rank stamp. |
| Text | 8 | Copy is typed in the edit, not generated. Names and terms follow Zenko's lore article. |
| Lore respect | 9 | Kurokawa rift, Resonance, vanguard / catalyst / anomaly, the digital manga, the TCG and the holder plot polls all come from Zenko's pinned article. The Shojo label for the kunai character is inferred. No mint, date or allocation claims. |
| Pitch elements | 9 | Sample WL leaderboard, the bots line, an X post mock-up with a result card, the Zenko title and the current-brand Encore end card. |

**Strict gate (every score 8+, art and motion 9+):** art passes at 9. Motion stays at a provisional 8 because full-speed perceptual review wasn't possible here.
